# TECHNIQUE/YEAR HARD RULES:
Publication year acts as a strict limiter for architecture classes. Never guess architectures not supported by the publication era:
- <2012: dl_cnn_*: false, dl_transformer: false, ml_traditional or classic_cv_based may apply.
- 2012-2016: ml_traditional, classic_cv_based, dl_cnn_classifier, dl_cnn_detector may apply. dl_transformer: false.
- ≥2017: All DL classes possible. dl_transformer: true only if transformer/attention/ViT/DETR/etc. is named or clearly described.
